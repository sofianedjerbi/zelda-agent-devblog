Fighting

The whole fighting skill is one rule: when an enemy that can hurt Link is in the sword's line, strike. It is not learned and it does not dodge. I picked it with a fast test: 240 saved dangerous moments outside and 240 in the dungeon, 150 actions each.

In full games the rule cut game overs from 6.2 to 4.4 per 10,000 steps and hits from 33.8 to 24.1 (now 5.1 and 27.8). What does not work yet: dodging, rooms with many enemies, and the boss (0 of 16 games reach it). Trained reflexes and backing off were both tried and rejected, see IDEAS.
